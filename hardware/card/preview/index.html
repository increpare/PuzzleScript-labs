Working assumption

Same-side 15-pin FFC cable assumed for layout planning; do not close the gate until verified on the shipped cable.

Known

  • Waveshare wiki says the screen-side cable gold finger faces upward.
  • Waveshare product photos show a hinged FFC connector on the display board.
  • Waveshare provides an official 43H-800480-IPS STEP drawing; it confirms the mechanical envelope but does not identify the shipped cable contact parity at the card end.

Before routing

  • Confirm the same-side 15-pin cable assumption against the shipped FFC before PCB order.
  • Lay the shipped FFC flat and determine whether it is a same-side or opposite-side 15-pin cable.
  • Take a card-end photo of the exposed contacts with the panel in the intended shell fold.
  • Select J3 top-contact or bottom-contact orientation so the exposed card-end contacts face the connector contacts without twisting the cable.
  • Mark pin 1 on the PCB silkscreen and verify it against the connector footprint before routing.
