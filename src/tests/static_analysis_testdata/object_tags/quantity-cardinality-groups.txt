title Static Analysis Object Tag Quantity Cardinality Groups

(Each property starts with one member per level.
 Hero: swaps between its members -> exactly one.
 Ghost: a member may be destroyed -> at most one.
 Clone: a member may be added next to another -> neither.
 Split: members on different layers; SplitA becomes SplitB, which may
 land on a cell already holding SplitB -> at most one.
 Phantom: in no level; its members only swap -> exactly zero.)
