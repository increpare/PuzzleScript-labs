A mixed-initiative level designer for PuzzleScript. You edit; the computer keeps checking solvability and proposes variations that its solver finds harder.

The loop

  1. Edit the level (left-drag paints, right-drag erases, Alt-click picks up, Shift-drag replaces the whole tile).
  2. Lock the parts you like so transforms leave them alone.
  3. Choose or write a transform, press Generate. Hover suggestions to preview the change; click to adopt.
  4. With Continue from pick on, each pick becomes the new starting point. The history strip lets you go back and branch.
  5. Simplify strips objects the solution doesn't need (window dressing) without changing the shortest solution.

Transform language

PuzzleScript rewrite rules plus choose, option and or:

[Target no Crate] -> [Target Crate]     (everywhere)
option 0.4 [Wall] -> []                  (each match, p=0.4)
choose 5 [Wall] -> [Crate]               (5 random matches)
choose 2-6 [Wall] -> []                  (2 to 6 of them)
choose 20 option 0.4 [Wall] -> []
or option 0.6 [no Wall no Crate] -> [Wall]   (weighted either/or)
choose 1 [Crate][Target] -> [][]         (two separate tiles)
choose 9 horizontal [Player | no Wall] -> [ | Player]

What the numbers mean

  • Effort: how many states the solver had to explore (minimum over weighted A*, greedy and BFS, as in the original MIS). Bigger is (usually) harder. It's a proxy — trust your eyes.
  • Moves: shortest solution when BFS could prove it, otherwise ≈ the length found.
  • Harder than current / min: the rate the transform finds levels the solver rates harder than yours. If it stalls, lock more of the level or make the level smaller — that's what raised it in the MIS user study.
